# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plus a little vanilla JS), so the page can be ported into the existing WordPress/Elementor site later.

## Users

Students whose school sets a minimum TOEIC score for graduation, candidates for parallel admission to a master's program or a school, employees with a professional project (mobility, promotion, a client-facing role) and job seekers. All of them have a score to reach by a given date, and most are in Paris or Île-de-France.

## Product Purpose

TOEIC First (France Prépa, trade name of NEXTCAP) runs TOEIC preparation courses aimed at a target score: a dedicated teacher at 1 place de la République (Paris 3e) or by video call, graded practice tests and follow-up until test day. The Paris landing page succeeds when a visitor asks for a callback from an advisor.

## Positioning

"France Prépa, l'excellence depuis 1982." Individual courses at the hours that suit the student, 7 days a week from 8 am to 9 pm, with graded practice tests. CPF funding is available through the TOEIC 4-skills Certification (RS7229).

## Operating Context

- The test is taken separately, at an ETS-approved centre (ETS France, 24 rue Chauchat, Paris 9e, or another centre in Île-de-France), for €189 (€17 off for students and job seekers).
- The visitor compares formats (individual, intensive course, remote group), checks CPF funding, then asks for a callback.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Programs: private lessons (10 to 100 h, 3 to 12 practice tests), individual intensive course (10 to 40 h, 1 to 2 weeks), remote group courses over 6 months (TEAM, DYNAMIC, BOOSTER), and AUTONOMIC (platform only, 3 months).
- No prices for the programs on the current page. Do not invent any.
- Score guarantee only on DYNAMIC and BOOSTER, subject to conditions.
- Primary CTA: "Être rappelé" (form at https://preparation-toeic.net/contact/#wpforms-form-1389). Secondary: phone 09 78 45 00 08.

## Brand Commitments

- "TF" logo with a swash (orange T, navy F), wordmark "TOEIC" in orange and "First" in navy.
- Colors to keep: navy #132845 and orange #e45635.
- Voice: factual, plain, sourced (ETS data marked "relevées en octobre 2026"), no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo: wp-content/uploads/2026/10/logo-toeic-first-transparent.png. CPF badge: pastille-cpf.png.

## Product Principles

1. Start from the visitor's deadline and target score.
2. Every figure is sourced and dated.
3. Make choosing a program easy: compare, don't overwhelm.
4. A callback is always one click away.
